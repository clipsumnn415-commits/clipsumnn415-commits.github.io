Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026.

StarNet Clipper is a personal tool operated by Clip Sumnn for its own accounts only.

What it accesses

When Clip Sumnn signs in with Google (YouTube) or TikTok, the tool uses that permission only to upload Clip Sumnn's own video clips to Clip Sumnn's own account and to read view counts and basic account details for those videos.

What it stores

Only the sign-in token needed to post, kept on Clip Sumnn's own computer in the macOS Keychain, plus a local list of uploaded videos.

What it does not do

It does not collect data about other people, does not sell or share any data, does not use data for advertising, and does not transfer data to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Clip Sumnn can revoke access at any time in Google Account settings (Security, Third-party access) or in TikTok settings, and can delete the local data.
